Privacy policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit this website and how it is used.

Information we collect

This website has no forms and no accounts, so it does not ask you for any personal information. It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Website hosting

Like most websites, this site is served by a hosting provider that keeps standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you visit and the time of your visit. These logs are used to keep the site running and secure.

Fonts

The site's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. Google's handling of this information is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Booking

When you select “Check availability” or “Book now”, you leave this website and go to a separate booking page. Any information you enter there, such as your name, contact details or payment details, is handled under that page's own privacy policy.

If you contact us

If you get in touch with us, we use your details only to reply to you and to arrange your stay. We do not sell your personal information.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.

Questions

If you have a question about your privacy, please get in touch through the villa's booking page.
